# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional learning space for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be constructive and respectful in feedback.
- Focus on the code, the lesson, or the idea, not the person.
- Ask questions clearly and assume positive intent.
- Keep examples and discussion relevant to SQL learning.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, threats, or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or learning sessions.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when behavior harms the community.

## Reporting
If you see behavior that violates this code of conduct, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately.
